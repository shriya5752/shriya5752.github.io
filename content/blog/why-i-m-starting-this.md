This is me, roughly four, sitting in front of a laptop I definitely could not operate, grinning like I’d already figured it out. I hadn’t figured out anything. I think I just found the game of poker, which in my head I was winning.

Funny enough, that photo is a more accurate prediction of my life than what came next. Because for most of my life (more specifically in 9th grade), I actually wanted to be a neurosurgeon.

Not in a vague “I like helping people” way. In a very specific, slightly unhinged “I watched every episode of The Good Doctor and decided this was my personality now” way. I wanted to understand the brain. Why people do the things they do, why we get stuck the way we get stuck, and why two people can go through the same thing and come out completely different.

That didn’t end up being the path. Turns out four-year-old me was onto something after all, because I’m a second-year CS student now. Actually third. SHIT. (don’t blame me this is the summer where i’m questioning things way more than i should be) But here’s the thing I didn’t expect, I never actually stopped trying to understand how things work, I just kept finding new things to point that at. brains, algorithms, whatever.

I learn random things all the time and then just forget I ever learned them. No real place to put any of it, which has been bothering me for a while now. That’s basically the whole reason this exists.

This is going to be an “I nerded out on something, here’s what I found” kind of blog. I’m going to chase whatever made me go wait, what, sometimes for way longer than the topic probably deserved, and then write up the chase. Could be a psychology concept that explains why you keep doing the thing you keep doing. Could be some random bit of science or internet history. Could be why an algorithm is named what it is. I’m a CS student, so every so often it’ll genuinely just be me three hours deep into some machine learning rabbit hole going wait, this is just a human thing wearing math, and dragging you down there with me.

No fixed theme beyond that. Whatever I got temporarily obsessed with that week has a home here now.

If you’ve never read anything I’ve written before, hi. I like understanding why things work, and I think most explanations are way more boring than the actual thing deserves to be.

Let’s see what we find.
